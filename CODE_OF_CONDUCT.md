# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background or skill level. This is a
technical project about security testing; disagreement about technique is
welcome, personal attacks are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise and assume good faith.
- Critique the code, the finding or the documentation, not the person.
- Respect scope and authorization boundaries in every discussion.
- Do not ask for or share credentials, private program details or other people's
  data.
- Do not post exploit code for systems you do not own or have permission to test.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Encouraging or facilitating unauthorized access.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or low-effort AI-generated noise.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for serious or
repeated violations. Reports can be sent through GitHub's private reporting
mechanisms; they will be handled confidentially.
